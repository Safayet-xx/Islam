Comparing Machine Learning Approaches Across Real-World Data

A group project comparing seven machine learning approaches on three very different problems: recognising human activity from sensor data, detecting phishing URLs, and trading a stock. The aim was to see how classical, neural, logic-based and reinforcement learning methods hold up when the data and the task change.

Context
MSc Data Science, University of Surrey, 2026
Team
6 members
Methods
7 approaches, 3 datasets

Datasets

UCI HAR

6-class activity recognition: sit, stand, walk, bike, stairs up and down.

PhiUSIIL

Phishing URL detection, around 235K rows and 54 features.

Yahoo Finance (AAPL)

8 years of daily price data used as a trading environment.

The agent underperformed a simple buy-and-hold strategy. This was a useful result in itself: it showed how sensitive tabular Q-Learning is to reward design and state representation when the signal in financial data is noisy.
