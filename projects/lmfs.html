London Muslim Funeral Services (LMFS)

A community platform for funeral support and donations, built for London's Bangladeshi Muslim community.

Audience
London's Bangladeshi Muslim community
Stack
Django, PostgreSQL
Tests
67 automated tests, all passing

Overview

LMFS is a web application that helps families arrange funeral support, lets supporters donate online or by bank transfer, and connects people during bereavement. I took a basic starter project and rebuilt it into a production-ready platform.

Key features

Online donations

Supporters can donate as guests or as registered members.

Secure card payments

Handled through a third-party payment gateway, with server-side confirmation.

Bank transfer

An alternative to card payments, with a dedicated contact and details page.

Receipts and emails

A PDF receipt and confirmation email are generated automatically after every donation.

User accounts

Login, registration and email verification.

Community-focused design

A fully rebranded, responsive interface designed for a community audience.

What I did

  • Designed and built the donation system end to end, from the form to payment confirmation and receipt.
  • Integrated a secure payment API and kept card handling off the server.
  • Wrote 67 automated tests covering payments, donations, accounts and views, all passing.
  • Hardened the settings for production: secret management, HTTPS, secure cookies and debug off.
  • Prepared the app for cloud deployment with a custom domain.

Outcome

A complete, tested platform ready to go live, giving the community a simple and trusted way to support families at a difficult time.

Skills shown

Full-stack Django development, payment integration, database design, automated testing, web security, deployment readiness, and building software for a real community need.
