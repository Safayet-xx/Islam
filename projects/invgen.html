Invgen: Multi-Tenant Invoicing SaaS

A full-stack invoicing platform that lets freelancers and small businesses create branded invoices and quotes, get paid by card or bank transfer, and keep their VAT and accountant paperwork in order, with teams and subscription plans built in.

Key features

Multi-tenancy

Every record is scoped to an organization, with team invites and a join flow. A platform super-admin console handles plans and tenants.

Documents

Invoices, quotes, credit notes and recurring "scheduled payment" templates. Line-item discounts, VAT, per-company branding (6 accent colours, two layouts, logo, bank details, QR payment link), PDF download, public share links, emailing and WhatsApp sending.

Payment ledger

Invoices can be paid in instalments. Balance and status (Unpaid, Partially Paid, Paid, Overdue) are always computed from the log. Corrections are reversal entries, never deletes, and manual and Stripe payments go through the same path. A payment log page makes it searchable.

Overdue automation

Opt-in late fees and emailed reminders from an hourly background job, built so fees and emails can't be applied twice.

Accounting

VAT return report with Excel export, accountant data exports, and client statements.

Billing

Free and paid plans with usage limits, expiry notices and renewals, and manual plan requests.

Dashboard

KPI cards and animated trend, pie and bar charts, plus a reminders list.

Engineering decisions

  1. Derived state over stored state

    Status and balance come from the payment ledger, so the data can't drift out of sync.

  2. Audit-safe corrections

    Reversal entries plus an audit log record every change instead of erasing history.

  3. Snapshot pattern

    Company and client details are copied onto an invoice when it is created, so editing a company later never rewrites an invoice you already sent.

  4. Idempotent background jobs

    The overdue sweep marks what it has done, so re-running it can't double-charge. It also never overwrites a "partially paid" status.

  5. Security-first free tier

    I found a bypass where deleting and recreating invoices reset the free-plan limit, and fixed it with a persistent counter. Invoice numbers are scoped per organization after I fixed a global-numbering bug.

  6. Two-mode schema management

    Development auto-migrates additively, and production is Alembic-only (16 migrations).

  7. Ops maturity

    I moved the app from Render (free-tier database expiry) to a self-hosted Docker VPS, with setup scripts, nightly backups and a data-import script.
